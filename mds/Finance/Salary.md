## Salary & Compensation Structure

Understanding the difference between what a company spends and what arrives in a bank account is critical for budgeting.

| Term                      | Definition                                                                           | Key Metric / Impact                                                                                    |
|---------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **CTC (Cost to Company)** | The total annual micro-economic cost an employer incurs to employ an individual.     | Includes gross salary, direct benefits, and statutory contributions.                                   |
| **Gross Salary**          | Total salary earned before any tax deductions or voluntary retirement contributions. | Excludes employer-paid benefits like gratuity or health insurance premiums.                            |
| **Net / In-Hand Salary**  | The actual cash amount credited to the bank account each month.                      | Calculated as: $\text{Gross Salary} - \text{TDS} - \text{EPF Contribution} - \text{Professional Tax}$. |

---

## Supplementary Compensation

* **ESOP (Employee Stock Ownership Plan):** Rights given to employees to purchase a specific number of company shares at
  a pre-determined price (exercise price) after a specific timeframe.
* **Vesting Period:** The mandatory duration an employee must remain with the company before they gain ownership of
  their allocated ESOPs.
* **Cliff:** The minimum period (usually one year) an employee must complete before any portion of their ESOPs vest.
* **Perquisites (Perks):** Non-cash benefits provided by an employer (e.g., company-provided accommodation, fuel
  reimbursement, or gadgets) that carry specific tax liabilities.

---

## Taxation & Payroll Deductions

* **TDS (Tax Deducted at Source):** The monthly income tax deducted directly by the employer based on the individual's
  projected annual income slab and deposited with the tax authority.
* **Form 16:** A mandatory certificate issued by the employer at the end of the financial year. It details the total
  salary paid and the exact amount of TDS deducted, serving as the foundational document for filing an Income Tax Return
  (ITR).
* **Financial Year (FY) vs. Assessment Year (AY):** The FY is the period in which income is actively earned (e.g., April
  1 to March 31). The AY is the subsequent year in which that income is evaluated and taxed.
* **Tax Exemptions:** Specific components of a salary structure (such as House Rent Allowance or Leave Travel Allowance)
  that are completely excluded from taxable income, subject to specific compliance rules.
* **Tax Deductions:** Allowable reductions to total taxable gross income achieved by utilizing specific investment
  instruments or incurring valid expenses (e.g., tuition fees, home loan principal).

---

## Statutory & Retirement Benefits

* **EPF (Employees' Provident Fund):** A mandatory savings scheme where both the employee and the employer contribute an
  equal percentage (typically 12% of the basic salary) toward a retirement corpus.
* **Gratuity:** A statutory benefit paid by an employer as a lump sum to an employee when they leave the organization,
  provided they have completed a minimum of 5 years of continuous service.
* **NPS (National Pension System):** A voluntary, defined-contribution retirement savings scheme regulated by the
  government, allowing exposure to equity and debt markets with an additional tax deduction lock-in.

---

## Core Investment & Banking Products

* **Emergency Fund:** A liquid cash reserve equivalent to 3 to 6 months of mandatory living expenses, strictly reserved
  for unplanned events like job loss or medical crises.
* **Liquid Funds:** A category of debt mutual funds investing in short-term market instruments (maturities up to 91
  days). They function as an alternative to savings accounts, offering high liquidity with slightly optimal yields.
* **Mutual Funds:** An investment vehicle that pools capital from multiple investors to purchase a diversified portfolio
  of stocks, bonds, or short-term debt.
* **SIP (Systematic Investment Plan):** An automated investment method where a fixed financial amount is directed into a
  selected mutual fund at regular intervals (usually monthly), utilizing dollar-cost/rupee-cost averaging.
* **PPF (Public Provident Fund):** A long-term, government-backed sovereign savings instrument with a fixed lock-in
  period (typically 15 years), offering tax-free interest and maturity amounts.

---

## Insurance & Risk Management

* **Term Insurance:** A pure life insurance product providing a fixed financial payout to nominees only if the insured
  individual passes away during the policy term. It accumulates no cash or maturity value, making it highly
  cost-effective.
* **Health Insurance (Mediclaim):** An insurance policy covering hospitalization and medical expenses. Relying solely on
  a corporate group health policy is a risk, as coverage ceases immediately upon resignation or termination. Personal
  health cover acts as the primary safety net.

## 1. Overview & Core Metrics

```
Cost to Company (CTC) [Total Package]
├── Retirements & Employer Benefits (Deducted from CTC before Gross)
│   ├── Employer EPF Contribution (12% of Basic)
│   ├── Gratuity Provision (4.81% of Basic)
│   └── Group Health/Life Insurance Premium (Company-sponsored)
│    
└── Gross Salary (Earnings Baseline)
    ├── Earnings Components (The Additions)
    │   ├── Basic Salary (≥50% of CTC)
    │   ├── House Rent Allowance (HRA) (40% or 50% of Basic)
    │   ├── Special Allowance (Fully taxable balancing figure)
    │   └── Reimbursements (Tax-exempt against bills: Internet, Fuel)
    │
    └── Mandatory Deductions (Subtracted from Gross to get Net In-Hand)
        ├── Employee EPF Contribution (12% of Basic)
        ├── Professional Tax (PT) (State tax up to ₹2,500/year)
        ├── Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) (Monthly Income Tax)
        │   ├── Old Tax Regime (With exemptions)
        │   └── New Tax Regime (Lower slab rates)
        └── Employee Insurance Deduction (Co-pay / Top-up)

```

### Key Salary Terms

* **Cost to Company (CTC):** The total financial cost an employer incurs for an employee per year, including Gross Salary and Employer Contributions (PF, Gratuity, Insurance).
* **Gross Salary:** Total earnings before any employee deductions. Calculated as:
$$\text{Gross Salary} = \text{CTC} - \text{Employer Retirements \& Benefits}$$


* **Net / Take-Home Salary:** The final amount credited to your bank account monthly. Calculated as:
$$\text{Net Salary} = \text{Gross Salary} - \text{Mandatory Deductions}$$



---

## 2. Earnings Components

Earnings are fixed or variable payouts that form your Gross Salary:

* **Basic Salary:** The core of your pay structure. It must be **$\ge$50% of Total CTC**, is fully taxable, and serves as the baseline for EPF and Gratuity calculations.
* **House Rent Allowance (HRA):** Provided to meet rented accommodation expenses.
* **Non-Metros:** 40% of Basic Salary.
* **Metros:** 50% of Basic Salary.
* **Tax Status:** Tax-exempt under Section 10(13A) with valid rent receipts, **only under the Old Tax Regime**.


* **Special Allowance:** A fully taxable balancing component used by employers to adjust the remainder of the CTC.
* **Reimbursements:** Tax-exempt payouts against actual bills submitted (e.g., fuel, internet, books, gadgets).
* **Conveyance & Leave Travel Allowance (LTA):** Covers travel expenses for work commuting or family travel within India (subject to specific tax-exemption limits under Old Tax Regime).
* **Bonus / Variable Pay:** Performance-linked payments (annual/quarterly) that are fully taxable.

---

## 3. Deductions Breakdown

Deductions are subtracted from Gross Salary to compute Net Take-Home Pay:

| Deduction | Description | Limit / Calculation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Employee EPF Contribution** | Mandatory retirement savings pool. | 12% of Basic Salary |
| **Professional Tax (PT)** | State-level tax levied on salaried employees. | Max ₹2,500 / year |
| **Tax Deducted at Source (TDS)** | Monthly income tax deducted based on projected annual liability. | Varies by tax regime & slab |
| **Health / Group Insurance** | Employee portion for corporate top-ups, co-pays, or parental coverage. | Actual premium opted |

## 4. Employee Provident Fund (EPF) Deep-Dive

EPF is a mandatory retirement scheme managed by the **Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO)** under the Ministry of Labour and Employment.

### Monthly Contribution Breakdown

For a mandatory rate of 12% of `Basic Salary + Dearness Allowance (DA)`:

| Contributor | Total Rate | Allocation Breakdown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Employee** | 12% | **12.00%** $\rightarrow$ EPF Account (Accumulates interest) |
| **Employer** | 12% | **3.67%** $\rightarrow$ EPF Account<br>

<br>**8.33%** $\rightarrow$ EPS Pension Scheme *(Capped at ₹1,250/mo on ₹15,000 basic cap)* |

---

### EPF Sub-Schemes Overview

#### 1. EPF (Provident Fund Account)

* **Returns:** Fixed annual interest declared by EPFO (8.25% for FY 2025–26).
* **Asset Allocation:** ~85–90% in Government & Debt Securities; ~10–15% in Equity Instruments.
* **Interest Rules:** Interest compounds annually and accrues even if you switch or leave jobs without immediate contributions.

#### 2. EPS (Employees' Pension Scheme)

* **Structure:** A defined benefit pension paying out monthly post-retirement (age 58) after completing at least 10 years of service.
* **Formula:**
$$\text{Monthly Pension} = \frac{\text{Average Pensionable Salary} \times \text{Pensionable Service}}{70}$$



*(Note: Pensionable salary is capped at ₹15,000/month for most employees).*

#### 3. EDLI (Employees' Deposit Linked Insurance)

* **Benefit:** Provides automatic life insurance coverage up to **₹7 Lakh** to the nominee in the event of an employee’s death during active service.

---

### Managing EPF Access & Transfers

Your **Universal Account Number (UAN)** is a 12-digit permanent identifier that remains unchanged throughout your career.

* **Primary Access Portals:**
1. **EPFO Passbook Portal:** `[https://passbook.epfindia.gov.in/](https://passbook.epfindia.gov.in/)` (Detailed breakdown of employee, employer, and pension balances).
2. **UMANG Mobile App:** Official government app for accessing mobile EPF services.


* **Prerequisites for Online Services:**
* Active UAN on the Unified Member Portal.
* Verified KYC Seeding (Aadhaar, PAN, and Bank Account linked to UAN).
* Registered mobile number linked to Aadhaar for OTP verification.


* **Job Switch Auto-Transfer:**
1. Provide your existing UAN to the new employer via **Form 11** during onboarding.
2. Upon the first PF deposit by the new employer, the EPFO system detects the link.
3. Balance automatically transfers from the previous Member ID to the new Member ID if your UAN is Aadhaar-verified.

## 5. Equity Compensation: RSUs & ESOPs

### Restricted Stock Units (RSUs)

RSUs represent a company's commitment to grant shares at a future date upon meeting specific conditions.

```
Grant Date ──(No Value / Tax-Free)──> Vesting Date ──(Taxable Event at FMV)──> Deposit into Brokerage Account

```

#### Key Lifecycle Stages:

1. **Grant:** Awarded a fixed number of units. No cash value, no voting rights, and zero tax liability.
2. **Vesting:** Fulfilling time-based or milestone constraints. *Example:* A 4-year schedule with a 1-year cliff (25% vests after 12 months, remaining 75% vests periodically over 3 years).
3. **Settlement:** RSUs convert into actual shares deposited into a designated brokerage account.

#### Tax & Regulatory Implications:

* **Vesting Event:** Vesting triggers a taxable income event calculated on the **Fair Market Value (FMV)** on the vest date.
* **Sell-to-Cover:** Employers often sell a portion of newly vested shares automatically to cover TDS liabilities.
* **Tax Filing (Foreign RSUs):** If foreign stock is held, filing under **ITR-2 or ITR-3** is mandatory to report Foreign Assets (Schedule FA), even if TDS was handled by payroll.
* **Cash-Settled RSUs:** In setups where cross-border equity transfers are restricted, the company liquidates the shares upon vesting, deducts Indian TDS, and disburses the net payout in INR via monthly payroll.

---

## 6. Glossary & Auxiliary Schemes

* **Dearness Allowance (DA):** A cost-of-living adjustment paid primarily to public sector employees and pensioners (often merged into Basic Salary in private IT sectors).
* **Voluntary Provident Fund (VPF):**
* An extension of EPF allowing salaried employees to contribute up to 100% of Basic Salary + DA.
* Earns the same interest rate as EPF (tax-free lump sum payout upon retirement).
* Employers do **not** match VPF contributions beyond the mandatory 12% EPF rate.
* Section 80C deductions apply under the **Old Tax Regime** (up to ₹1.5 Lakh limit). Under the **New Tax Regime**, 80C deductions are unavailable, but the high tax-free interest accrual remains attractive.


* **Public Provident Fund (PPF):** A sovereign, long-term savings scheme open to all Indian citizens (salaried and self-employed) with a 15-year lock-in period, separate from corporate salary structures.
